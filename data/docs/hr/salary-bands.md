# Salary bands and benefits (FY 2082/83)

Monthly gross salary bands in Nepali rupees. Bands were raised by 6% from Shrawan 2082.

| Grade | Typical roles | Monthly gross salary |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Helper, guard, cook | Rs 19,500 to Rs 26,000 |
| G2 | Operator, technician, driver | Rs 32,000 to Rs 48,000 |
| G3 | Junior engineer, accountant, HR officer | Rs 55,000 to Rs 80,000 |
| G4 | Engineer, shift in-charge, senior accountant | Rs 85,000 to Rs 1,25,000 |
| G5 | Plant manager, department head | Rs 1,40,000 to Rs 2,20,000 |
| G6 | Chief officers | Rs 2,80,000 to Rs 4,50,000 |

## Benefits

- Social Security Fund: the company contributes 20% of basic salary and the employee 11%.
- Dashain allowance: one month's basic salary, paid before Ghatasthapana.
- Site allowance: 15% of basic salary for staff based at Gongar.
- Insurance: medical cover of Rs 2 lakh and accident cover of Rs 15 lakh per employee.
- Annual bonus: under the Bonus Act, 10% of net profit is shared among staff. No bonus was paid for
  FY 2081/82 because the company did not make a net profit after interest.
