# Performance review cycle 2082

## Timeline

- Self-assessment opens: 1 Kartik 2082
- Manager reviews due: 30 Kartik 2082
- Calibration meeting of department heads: 7 Mangsir 2082
- Results and increments shared: 20 Mangsir 2082

## Ratings and increments

| Rating | Meaning | Increment on basic salary |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | 10% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | 7% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | 4% |
| 2 | Needs improvement | 0%, with an improvement plan |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | 0%, with a written warning |

No more than 15% of a department can be rated 5. The calibration meeting checks this.

## Notes for managers

Site staff are reviewed by the shift in-charge first, then by the Plant Manager. Ratings must be
backed by examples from the shift logs or project records.
