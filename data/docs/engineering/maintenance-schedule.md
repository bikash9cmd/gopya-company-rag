# Maintenance schedule

## Annual overhaul

The annual overhaul is done in the dry season, from 10 to 30 Chaitra, when river flow is lowest and
only one unit can run anyway. One unit at a time is overhauled, so the plant never stops fully.
The budget for the Chaitra 2083 overhaul is held by Finance.

During the overhaul the team inspects the runner for sediment erosion, replaces the guide vane
bushings and tests the protection relays.

## Runner repair

Sediment wears the turbine runners quickly. Each runner is taken out for hard-coating repair every
two years. The company keeps one spare runner. A second spare runner is planned for FY 2082/83.

## Routine checks

| Check | How often |
|---|---|
| Bearing temperature and vibration readings | Every shift |
| Desander gate operation test | Weekly |
| Battery bank and DC system test | Monthly |
| Transformer oil sample | Every 6 months |
| Penstock inspection walk | Every 3 months and after every earthquake above magnitude 5 |
