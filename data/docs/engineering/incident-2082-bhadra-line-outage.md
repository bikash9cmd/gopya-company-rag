# Incident report: 132 kV line outage, 3 to 9 Bhadra 2082

## What happened

At 02:40 on 3 Bhadra 2082, a landslide near Kharidhunga damaged two towers (tower 27 and tower 28)
of the 132 kV line between Gongar and Singati. The line tripped and all three units went to
no-load. After 30 minutes without the line, the shift in-charge stopped all units.

## Response

The line contractor reached the site on 4 Bhadra. A temporary bypass line was built and the line
was restored at 18:10 on 9 Bhadra. The plant was back at full load by 19:30.

## Impact

The plant lost about 6 days of generation. SCADA records of water availability were sent to Finance
for the deemed energy claim. No one was injured.

## Actions

1. Ask NEA to add slope protection at towers 27 and 28 before the next monsoon.
2. Keep an emergency stock of tower parts at Gongar.
3. Add the line route to the daily monsoon status issued by the safety officer.
