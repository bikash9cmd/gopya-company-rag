# Plant operations manual (summary)

## Units

The powerhouse has three horizontal Francis units of 8 MW each, rated at a net head of 182 m and a
design discharge of 5.2 m³/s per unit. The generators are 11 kV and connect to the 132 kV switchyard
through two step-up transformers.

## Starting a unit

1. Check the intake gate is open and the forebay level is above 1,642 m.
2. Open the main inlet valve with the bypass first, then the main valve.
3. Start the unit from the SCADA screen in automatic mode. The unit synchronises with the grid in
   about 4 minutes.
4. Load the unit in steps of 2 MW, waiting at least 2 minutes between steps.

## Sediment shutdown procedure

The intake has an online turbidity meter. Sediment is measured in parts per million (ppm).

| Sediment at intake | Action |
|---|---|
| Below 3,000 ppm | Normal operation |
| 3,000 to 5,000 ppm | Flush the desander every 2 hours and inform the Plant Manager |
| Above 5,000 ppm | Reduce to one unit and flush continuously |
| Above 7,000 ppm | Shut down all units and close the intake gate |

Restart only after sediment stays below 4,000 ppm for 2 hours. Record every shutdown in the shift
log with the time, ppm reading and the name of the shift in-charge.

## Grid trips

If the 132 kV line trips, the units go to no-load automatically. Inform the NEA load dispatch centre
and keep the units at speed-no-load for up to 30 minutes. If the line is not restored in 30 minutes,
stop the units and record the outage start time for the deemed energy claim.
