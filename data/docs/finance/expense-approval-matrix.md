# Expense approval matrix

| Amount (per purchase) | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 50,000 | Head of department |
| Rs 50,001 to Rs 5 lakh | Head of department and CFO |
| Rs 5 lakh to Rs 50 lakh | CFO and CEO |
| Above Rs 50 lakh | Board of directors |

Purchases above Rs 5 lakh need three written quotations. Purchases above Rs 20 lakh need a sealed
tender published in a national daily, as required by the company's procurement rules.

Emergency purchases needed to restore generation can be made by the Plant Manager up to Rs 10 lakh
and approved after the fact by the CFO within 7 days.

VAT of 13% applies to most purchases. Finance deducts TDS at 1.5% on supplier payments for goods
and 15% on service fees paid to individuals, as required by the Income Tax Act.
