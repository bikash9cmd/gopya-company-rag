# Q1 results FY 2082/83 (Shrawan to Ashwin 2082)

## Generation

The plant generated 49.8 GWh in Q1, against a plan of 54.0 GWh. The shortfall of 4.2 GWh came
from two causes:

- A 132 kV line outage between Gongar and Singati from 3 to 9 Bhadra after a landslide tripped
  two towers. NEA could not take power for 6 days. A deemed energy claim of 3.1 GWh was filed on
  20 Bhadra.
- A sediment shutdown of 26 hours on 18 Shrawan, when sediment at the intake reached 7,400 ppm.

## Revenue

Revenue from energy sales was Rs 26.0 crore, against a plan of Rs 28.2 crore. If the deemed energy
claim is accepted, Rs 1.6 crore will be added.

## Expenses

Operating expenses were Rs 6.1 crore, in line with the budget. Emergency tower repair support of
Rs 38 lakh was paid to the line contractor and will be claimed from insurance.

## Cash

Cash in the bank at the end of Ashwin was Rs 14.3 crore. The next loan instalment of Rs 10.5 crore
is due on 15 Poush.
