# Power purchase agreement and tariff

## Agreement

The company sells all energy from the Kanchan Khola plant to NEA under a 30-year power purchase
agreement (PPA) signed on 12 Falgun 2074. The agreement runs from COD until Mangsir 2109.

## Tariff

| Season | Months | Base tariff |
|---|---|---|
| Dry season | Poush to Chaitra (mid-December to mid-April) | Rs 8.40 per kWh |
| Wet season | Baisakh to Mangsir (mid-April to mid-December) | Rs 4.80 per kWh |

The base tariff escalates by 3% of the base rate each year for the first 8 years after COD
(simple escalation, not compounded). In FY 2082/83 the plant is in its third escalation year, so
the tariff is Rs 9.16 per kWh in the dry season and Rs 5.23 per kWh in the wet season.

## Contract energy

The PPA sets a contract energy of 138.6 GWh a year, of which 31.2 GWh is dry-season energy. If the
plant generates less than 80% of the monthly contract energy for reasons within its control, NEA
can charge a penalty on the shortfall.

## Deemed energy

When the plant is ready to generate but NEA cannot take the power, for example because of a
transmission line outage, NEA pays for the energy that could have been generated (deemed energy).
Deemed energy claims must be filed with NEA within 30 days of the outage with SCADA records.
