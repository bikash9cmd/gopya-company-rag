# Budget for fiscal year 2082/83

The fiscal year runs from 1 Shrawan 2082 to the end of Asar 2083. The board approved this budget on
25 Asar 2082. All amounts are in Nepali rupees.

## Revenue

Expected generation is 141 GWh, of which 30 GWh in the dry season. Expected revenue from energy
sales is Rs 87.4 crore.

## Operating expenses

| Item | Budget |
|---|---|
| Staff salaries and benefits | Rs 9.8 crore |
| Operation and maintenance | Rs 6.2 crore |
| Annual overhaul (Chaitra 2083) | Rs 2.9 crore |
| Insurance | Rs 1.6 crore |
| Royalty to the government | Rs 3.1 crore |
| Local benefit sharing and CSR | Rs 0.9 crore |
| Head office and administration | Rs 1.4 crore |
| Total operating expenses | Rs 25.9 crore |

## Debt service

The project loan from the bank consortium led by a commercial bank has Rs 182 crore outstanding at
the start of the year. Principal repayment this year is Rs 21 crore and interest is about Rs 17.5
crore, at the consortium's base rate plus 2%.

## Capital expenditure

Rs 4.5 crore is reserved for a new desander flushing gate and the second spare runner for the
turbines.

## Dividend

No dividend is planned for FY 2082/83. The board will review this after the Q2 results.
