# IT and information security

## Accounts and passwords

Every employee gets one company account. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are changed
every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for email and the finance portal.

## Remote access

Use the company VPN to reach the HR portal, finance portal and SCADA historian from outside the
office. VPN access for SCADA is given only to engineering staff and needs approval from the Head
of Engineering and Operations.

## Phishing and suspicious messages

Do not open attachments from unknown senders. Forward suspicious emails to it-help@kanchanhydro.example
and then delete them. If you clicked a link or entered your password, call the IT desk on
extension 210 immediately, day or night.

## Confidential information

Documents are classified by department. You may only share a document with people in the same
department or with management. Salary information, personal identity numbers and bank details
are confidential and are handled only by HR. Never paste company documents into public AI tools.

## Internal assistant

Gopya is the company's internal assistant. It only answers from documents your role is allowed to
see. If Gopya cannot find something, ask the document owner listed at the top of each policy.
