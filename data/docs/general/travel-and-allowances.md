# Travel and daily allowance policy

## Daily allowance (DA)

| Where you travel | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Kathmandu valley (for site staff) | Rs 2,500 per night |
| Gongar site (for head office staff) | Rs 1,800 per night |
| Other districts in Nepal | Rs 2,200 per night |
| India | INR 3,000 per night |

The daily allowance covers food and incidental costs. Hotels outside the site camp are booked by
Admin and paid directly by the company, up to Rs 4,500 per night.

## Travel between Kathmandu and site

The company runs a staff jeep between Baluwatar and Gongar every Sunday and Thursday, leaving
Baluwatar at 6:00. Book a seat with Admin by 15:00 the day before. If you travel by public bus
instead, the company reimburses the bus fare against the ticket.

During the monsoon (Asar to Bhadra), check the road status with the site office before travelling:
the Singati to Gongar road is often closed by landslides.

## Claims

Submit travel claims within 15 days of returning, with receipts, in the finance portal. Claims
submitted later than 30 days are not paid. Advances must be settled within 7 days of returning.
