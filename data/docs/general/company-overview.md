# Company overview

Kanchan Hydro Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional independent power producer used to demonstrate Gopya.
Every person, number and project in this knowledge base is made up.

## The plant

The company owns and operates the Kanchan Khola Hydroelectric Project, a 24 MW run-of-river plant
in Dolakha district, Bagmati Province. The plant has three Francis turbine units of 8 MW each.
Water is diverted at the headworks near Lamabagar, passes through a 4.2 km headrace tunnel and
drops 182 m to the powerhouse at Gongar.

The plant reached its commercial operation date (COD) on 15 Mangsir 2079. Power is evacuated
over a 132 kV line to the Singati substation and sold to Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) under a
power purchase agreement.

## Offices

- Head office: Baluwatar, Kathmandu. Finance, HR and the management team sit here.
- Site office: Gongar powerhouse camp, Dolakha. Operations, maintenance and the safety team sit here.

The company has 86 staff: 22 at the head office and 64 at site. Site staff work in three shifts
because the powerhouse is staffed 24 hours a day.
