# Site safety rules

These rules apply to everyone at the Gongar powerhouse, the headworks and the tunnel, including
visitors from the head office.

## Personal protective equipment (PPE)

A helmet, safety boots and a high-visibility vest are required everywhere outside the camp. Ear
protection is required inside the powerhouse. Visitors collect PPE from the security gate.

## Tunnel entry

No one enters the headrace tunnel without a written tunnel entry permit signed by the Plant
Manager. A gas test must be done before entry, and at least two people must enter together. The
tunnel is entered only when all three units are shut down and the intake gate is closed and
locked.

## Monsoon protocol

From Jestha to Ashwin, the safety officer issues a daily landslide and flood status at 7:00.
When the status is red, all work at the headworks stops and staff move to the camp. If river
sediment at the intake goes above 5,000 ppm, operators follow the sediment shutdown procedure in
the plant operations manual.

## Reporting

Report every injury, near miss or unsafe condition to the safety officer within 24 hours. Emergency
phone at site: extension 100. The nearest hospital is Charikot Primary Health Centre, about two
hours by road.
