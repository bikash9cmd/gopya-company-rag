# Leave policy

This policy follows the Labour Act 2074 and applies to every permanent and contract employee.

## Home leave

Employees earn one day of home leave for every 20 days worked. Home leave can be accumulated up to
90 days. Unused home leave above 90 days is paid out at the basic salary rate at the end of the
fiscal year in Asar.

## Sick leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave a year. Sick leave of more than 3 days in a row needs a
doctor's certificate. Unused sick leave can be accumulated up to 45 days.

## Festival and public holidays

The company gives 13 public holidays a year, published by HR every Shrawan. Site staff who work
during Dashain or Tihar because the plant must run get a substitute day off and a festival duty
allowance of Rs 1,500 per day worked.

## Maternity and paternity leave

Female employees get 98 days of maternity leave, of which 60 days are fully paid. Male employees
get 15 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the birth.

## Mourning leave

Employees get 13 days of paid mourning leave (kriya bida) on the death of a parent or
parent-in-law.

## How to apply

Apply in the HR portal at least 7 days before planned leave. Your line manager approves leave of
up to 5 days. Leave of more than 5 days also needs approval from the head of department. Site
staff must also confirm shift cover with the shift in-charge before applying.
